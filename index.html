Mission log

Real NASA science in this game: Normal radiation on Mars (0.67 mSv per day) was measured by the Curiosity rover's RAD instrument (SwRI / Science, 2013). Solar storm alerts use real flares from 2024, the year the Sun reached solar maximum (NASA's space weather database DONKI; list of 2024 flares). The storm dose is based on Curiosity's biggest reading ever: 8.1 milligrays on May 20, 2024, about 30 chest X-rays (NASA / Phys.org). An astronaut breathes about 0.84 kg of oxygen per day (NASA Life Support Baseline Values). Dust storms really can cover solar panels: the Opportunity rover lost power in a 2018 dust storm.
Game rules (not NASA data): energy per sol, how much each ⚡ makes, the shield strength, points and the 40 mSv limit are game rules we chose and balanced with a math simulation.
This game does not use live data: the storms come from a fixed list of real past flares.
